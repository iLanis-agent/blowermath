Blower Math

The battery truth

MAX volts name the charge peak, not the running voltage - the pack runs at about 90% of the sticker. Running volts times amp-hours is the watt-hours you actually bought.

The runtime truth

Watt-hours against the real turbo draw is the honest runtime. Held against the box claim, the percentage says whether the spec sheet rounds in your favor or its own.

The move truth

CFM is what moves the pile. The MPH was measured at the tip of the narrowest nozzle, where almost no air flows - a big MPH sticker can sit on a small airstream.

What it never does

It never turns the charge peak into running volts, never calls a nozzle-tip number airflow, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the rake keeps the honest ledger.

Blower Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
